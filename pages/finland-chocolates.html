Heritage & Science

The Dammenberg™ Story in a Nut-Free Shell

Dammenberg’s story began in 1994 with a small chocolate factory founded by Marko Iso-Kungas, a diabetic food technology engineer, and his wife Kirsi, a nurse specializing in allergies.

With personal dedication and development, we became Europe’s first manufacturer focusing exclusively on nut-free, gluten-free, egg-free, and GMO-free chocolates. At Dammenberg, we treat chocolate passionately, with eternal love.

Today, our delicacies are enjoyed from the Nordic countries to Central Europe, the USA, Japan, and the Arabian Peninsula. Now, Drevon Group is securing the exclusive channel to bring this masterpiece to India.
