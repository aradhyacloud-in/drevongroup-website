Heritage & Science

The Dammenberg™ Story in a Nut-Free Shell

Dammenberg’s story began in 1994 with a small chocolate factory founded by Marko Iso-Kungas, a diabetic food technology engineer, and his wife Kirsi, a nurse specializing in allergies.

With personal dedication and development, they became Europe’s first manufacturer focusing exclusively on nut-free, gluten-free, egg-free, and GMO-free chocolates. At Dammenberg, they treat chocolate passionately, with eternal love.

Today, their delicacies are enjoyed from the Nordic countries to Central Europe, the USA, Japan, and the Arabian Peninsula. Now, Drevon Chocolates—operating as an exclusive subsidiary of the Drevon Group—is securing the sole channel to bring this European masterpiece to India's most discerning consumers.
