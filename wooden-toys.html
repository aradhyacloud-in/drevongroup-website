OUR HERITAGE

A Legacy Carved by Hand

At Drevon Group, we believe that true luxury lies in craftsmanship—the kind that cannot be replicated by machines. Our collection brings you the spirit of Channapatna, a town whose rhythmic wood-turnery has been celebrated for over 225 years.

Every piece in this collection is a tribute to the artisans who use traditional Aale Mara (ivory wood) and vibrant, non-toxic vegetable dyes to create toys that are as safe for your child as they are beautiful to behold.

We chose to bring these treasures to you not just to sell toys, but to protect a dying art form. In a world of mass-produced plastic, we invite you to choose play that is conscious, sustainable, and soulful.

When you bring a Drevon Channapatna toy into your home, you aren’t just getting a product; you are keeping a piece of Indian cultural heritage alive, supporting local livelihoods, and gifting your loved ones a connection to a simpler, more meaningful way to play.
