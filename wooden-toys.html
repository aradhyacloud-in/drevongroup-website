OUR HERITAGE

A Legacy Carved by Hand

At Drevon Wooden Toys, we believe true luxury is not created by machines, but by the human touch — by the patience, skill, and soul of artisans who dedicate generations to perfecting their craft.

Rooted in the historic town of Channapatna, our toys are inspired by an art form that has flourished for over two centuries. Every piece is handcrafted using traditional wood-turning techniques, sustainable Aale Mara wood, and vibrant natural dyes that are safe, timeless, and deeply connected to Indian heritage.

In a world dominated by plastic and mass production, we aim to preserve the warmth of mindful craftsmanship. Each toy is designed not only to inspire play, but to create emotional connection, curiosity, and lasting memories across generations.

By bringing a Drevon toy into your home, you are supporting artisan communities, preserving cultural legacy, and embracing a more conscious, meaningful way of living and gifting.
