Who We Are

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To emerge as a pivotal force in both domestic and global markets, driving India's industrial future by seamlessly fulfilling the critical raw material demands of advanced sectors, including semiconductor manufacturing, aerospace, and electric vehicle industries. We envision scaling the Drevon Group into a ₹1,000-crore enterprise with a formidable global footprint, setting the ultimate benchmark for ethical, transparent, and fair business practices across every industry we enter.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build a highly diversified, legally transparent, and quality-driven enterprise that serves society while delivering unmatched value to our clients. We actively seek to collaborate with highly enthusiastic, visionary partners to foster mutually beneficial, global business growth.

We achieve this through:

Industrial Foundation: Fulfilling the critical metal requirements of large-scale industries through the secure brokerage and supply of ultra-high-purity nickel wire.

Diversified Excellence: Delivering uncompromising quality across our additional business units, which include the preservation of cultural heritage through handcrafted wooden toys, strategic real estate brokerage, the upcoming launch of premium Finland chocolates, and forward-thinking technology consulting through Drevon Applied Intelligence.

Unwavering Integrity: Conducting every business transaction with absolute honesty, strict adherence to government laws, and a transparent, ethical framework.
