Drevon Metal Trading

Drevon Metal Trading is the industrial foundation of the Group. We broker and supply certified, high-purity metals to the sectors that cannot compromise on material quality, including semiconductor manufacturing, aerospace and electric vehicles. Every consignment is verified, every transaction documented and every partner treated with complete transparency.

  • Secure brokerage and supply of ultra-high-purity nickel
  • Verified, certified stockpiles with documented purity
  • Sourcing for large-scale industrial requirements
  • Legally compliant, ethical deal structuring

Vision, Mission & Values

Our Vision

To become a pivotal force in domestic and global markets by securing the critical raw materials behind semiconductor, aerospace and electric vehicle industries, while scaling Drevon Group into a ₹1,000-crore enterprise that sets the benchmark for ethical, transparent business.

We aim to drive India's industrial future by fulfilling the raw material demands of advanced sectors, alongside a technology arm that equips businesses with intelligent systems. With a formidable global footprint, we intend to set the ultimate benchmark for ethical, transparent and fair business practice in every industry we enter.

Our Mission

To build a transparent, quality-driven enterprise through two focused divisions: Drevon Metal Trading, supplying high-purity metals to large-scale industry, and Drevon Technologies, delivering consulting, ERP, AI and digital solutions to ambitious businesses worldwide.

We achieve this through:

Industrial Foundation: Fulfilling the critical metal requirements of large-scale industries through secure brokerage and supply of ultra-high-purity nickel wire.

Technology Excellence: Delivering consulting, custom ERP, AI tools and digital experiences through Drevon Technologies.

Unwavering Integrity: Conducting every transaction with absolute honesty, strict adherence to government laws and a transparent, ethical framework.
