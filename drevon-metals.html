What Drevon Metals does

Drevon Metals is the metals division of Drevon Group. We are metal brokers and suppliers serving large-scale industrial buyers across India.

Buyers in aerospace, energy storage, electronics and heavy manufacturing need metal they can trust: the right grade, the right purity and clean paperwork. We source from verified holders, check the material against laboratory reports, and manage the transaction from first enquiry to dispatch.

We work with nickel, titanium, copper and other industrial metals. Our approach is simple: transparent documentation, independent testing and direct communication with decision-makers on both sides of the deal.
